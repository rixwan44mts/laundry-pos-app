Laundry POS

Point-of-sale software for laundry and dry-cleaning shops — booking, tags, payments, pickup and daily cash registers, in English and Urdu.

What it does

  • Books orders, prints receipts and tag numbers, and tracks every order until pickup.
  • Keeps the shop's cash register, expenses, staff salaries and reports.
  • Runs on the shop's own computer and works without internet.

Backups to Google Drive

The shop owner can connect their own Google Drive. Laundry POS then saves an encrypted backup of the shop's data there every night, in a folder called LaundryPOS Backups.

  • It uses Google's drive.file permission: it can only see and manage the backup files it created — nothing else in the owner's Drive.
  • Backups are encrypted on the shop computer before upload. Only the shop owner's backup password or recovery key can open them.
  • The owner can disconnect at any time, in Laundry POS or in their Google Account settings.
